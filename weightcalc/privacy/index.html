WeightCalc Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

WeightCalc is a simple weight converter for iPhone. It does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data collection

The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. The numbers you type are used only to show the conversion on your screen and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Third parties

WeightCalc does not include any third-party services or SDKs, and no data is shared with anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
